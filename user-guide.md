## Phase 1: First-Time Setup

When you first log into MFinance, your dashboard will be empty. Follow these two steps to build your financial foundation.

### 1. Define Your Categories

Before you can track where your money goes, you need to create your categories.

1. Navigate to **Settings** via the sidebar (or hamburger menu on mobile).
2. Open the **Categories** tab and use the **Quick Add** tool.
3. Create your categories:

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| **Expense** | Groceries, Rent, DIU Tuition, Transport |
| **Income** | Salary, Freelance, Allowances |

---

### 2. Create Your Wallets (Accounts)

Tell MFinance where your money is actually stored.

1. Navigate to **Accounts**.
2. Click **+ Create Account** to add your real-world financial endpoints.

   > Examples: `Main Bank Account`, `bKash`, `Cash Wallet`

3. Once an account is created, click **+ Add Money** on the account card to deposit your current starting balance.

---

## Phase 2: Daily Operations

Once your accounts and categories are set up, MFinance handles the math automatically.

### 1. Recording Transactions

Whenever money enters or leaves your life, log it here.

1. Navigate to **Transactions** → click **New Entry**.
2. Choose **EXPENSE** or **INCOME**.
3. Select the **Account** the money is affecting and tag the **Category**.

> 🛡️ **Smart Safety Feature — Overdraft Protection**
> If you try to log a $50 expense from an account with only $20, MFinance will **block the transaction** to keep your financial data perfectly accurate.

---

### 2. Tracking Net Worth (The Dashboard)

The Dashboard is your real-time financial command center.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Current Balance** | Total liquid cash across all accounts |
| **Net Worth** | Total Liquid Cash + Money owed to you − Money you owe others |
| **Period Flow** | Track income and expenses over a custom date range via the **Date Filter** |
| **Export** | Download a CSV of filtered transactions for taxes or sharing |

## Phase 3: Advanced Financial Tools

### 1. Budgeting (Spending Goals)

Stop guessing how much you have left to spend this month.

1. Navigate to **Budgets**.
2. Click **Set Budget** on any expense category to define a monthly limit.
3. As you log transactions, the progress bar fills up automatically.

> ⚠️ MFinance alerts you when you cross the **90% threshold** so you can slow down your spending.

---

### 2. Managing Debts & Loans

MFinance uses **double-entry accounting** to ensure borrowed money is tracked flawlessly.

Navigate to **Debts** and choose the appropriate flow:

**People I Owe (Borrowing)**
- Log borrowed money here.
- The amount appears in your selected Account *(balance increases)*.
- Net Worth **stays the same** — tracked as a liability.

**Others Owe Me (Lending)**
- Log money lent to a friend here.
- The amount leaves your Account *(balance decreases)*.
- Net Worth **stays the same** — tracked as an asset.

**Making Repayments**
- Click **Make Payment / Record Receipt**.
- Select the exact Account sending or receiving the money.
- Wallet balances stay perfectly synced with your debt records.

---

### 3. Subscriptions (Recurring Costs)

Keep an eye on your automated bills.

1. Navigate to **Subscriptions**.
2. Add your recurring monthly or yearly charges.

   > Examples: `Netflix`, `Spotify`, `Gym`

3. Visualize your total fixed overhead at a glance every month.

## 📱 Mobile Experience

MFinance is fully responsive. On the go:

- Tap the **☰ Menu** icon (top right) to open the navigation drawer.
- Tap any **Dashboard card** to reveal advanced asset calculations and breakdowns.
- Log expenses **directly from your phone** while standing at the checkout counter.
